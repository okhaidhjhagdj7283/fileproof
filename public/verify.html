Verify a file

Paste a proof link or ID to look up a preserved file, then upload your copy to compare fingerprints.

Step 1 — Paste proof link or ID
Step 2 — Upload your file to compare

Your file is hashed locally in your browser — it is not sent to any server.

HOW IT WORKS
1. FileProof stores a SHA-256 fingerprint of the original file when a proof is created.
2. You upload your copy — the fingerprint is computed locally in your browser.
3. The two fingerprints are compared. A match means the files are identical.
FileProof does not prove that an event happened — only that a file matches the preserved original.
